# Sensitivity of the headline results to the parameter setting

Generated by `scripts/run_sensitivity.py` and summarised by
`scripts/summarise_sensitivity.py`, on 2026-10-25.

    python scripts/run_sensitivity.py --epochs 100 --jobs 6
    python scripts/measure_delta_power.py
    python scripts/summarise_sensitivity.py

Every committed result sits at one setting of four knobs. This sweeps each one at
a time, holding the others at the shipped value, and asks which conclusions
survive.

## Regime

N = 1000, k = 20, φ = 0.02, 100 epochs, five seeds (42–46). One knob moves per
axis; everything else is `experiments/base.yaml` as shipped.

| axis | levels swept | shipped |
|---|---|---|
| `population.taste_sigma` | 0.2, 0.5, 1.0 | 0.5 |
| `hypergamy.shock_scale` | 0.5, 1.5, 3.0 | 1.5 |
| `hypergamy.beta / alpha` | 1.0, 2.0, 3.0 | 2.0 |
| `hypergamy.delta` | 0.0, 0.5, 1.0 | 0.5 |

The shipped cell appears in three of the four axes and its numbers are identical
across them (+0.025973 gap, reach 0.9566), which is the check that the axes are
independent and that the reuse is correct.

## What holds, and what does not

**C1 — the NCF's exposure Gini exceeds the oracle's — holds in all 12 cells.**
Every cell's paired gap has a 95% interval excluding zero, all positive. The
smallest is `shock_scale` 0.5 at +0.0097 [0.0062, 0.0116]; the largest is
`shock_scale` 3.0 at +0.0470 [0.0192, 0.0665]. No cell reverses it. This is the
robust result of the whole sweep.

**C2 — the gap is small at 100 epochs — holds in all 12 cells** against a
ceiling of 0.05 Gini points fixed before the cells were read. The only cell that
comes close is `shock_scale` 3.0 at +0.0470, and its interval reaches 0.0665, so
at that noise level the "cold-start effect, nearly gone by epoch 100" reading is
the weakest it gets in this grid.

**C3 — the NCF reaches most agents — fails at `shock_scale` 0.5.** Reach there
is 0.4616 against 0.9566 at the shipped setting. This is the one real reversal in
the grid: at low chemistry noise the filter never learns coverage, so the
mechanism THESIS H3 offers for the gap closing (the filter learning who it can
represent) is not available. The gap is still small and still positive there —
+0.0097 — but it is small because both arms concentrate, not because the NCF
caught up.

**C4 — clearing is not degenerate — holds in all 12 cells.** The lowest clearing
rate is 0.0916, at the same `shock_scale` 0.5 cell, and its interval excludes
zero. Clearing is low there, not absent.

## The β/α axis: the documented guardrail does not reproduce at the shipped settings

`experiments/base.yaml` states, and `tests/cpp/test_delta_power.cpp` pins, that
δ has power only when β/α ≥ 2: below that the hypergamous indicator is a constant
across the shortlist. The overlap table in that test gives 0.9374 at β/α = 1.0,
0.7615 at 2.0 and 0.7366 at 3.0 — a clean monotone ordering.

Those numbers are measured at **taste_sigma = 0 and shock_scale = 0**. This
sweep runs at 0.5 and 1.5. Re-measuring at the swept settings (`delta_power.csv`,
δ = 1, top_k = 20, N = 1000) gives:

| β/α | overlap at taste_sigma=0, shock=0 | overlap at shipped taste_sigma/shock |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 0.9374 | **0.8440** |
| 2.0 | 0.7615 | **0.8540** |
| 3.0 | 0.7366 | **0.8674** |

The ordering **reverses**. At the shipped heterogeneity and noise, β/α = 1 gives
δ *more* power than β/α = 3, and all three levels sit above the 0.75 that the
guardrail treats as meaningful. The rule of thumb is a property of a
noise-free, homogeneous population and does not describe the population the
experiments actually run.

**Consequence for the claim.** The δ axis results are therefore not useless, but
they cannot be read as "δ matters above β/α = 2 and not below". What the sweep
does show is that the gap is flat across δ: +0.0229 at δ = 0, +0.0260 at 0.5 and
+0.0263 at 1.0, a spread of 0.0034 against intervals roughly 0.012 wide. So δ
does not move the headline, whatever its power — and the β/α axis moves it more
(0.0116 across its levels) than δ does.

## What a reader may conclude

- The NCF-versus-oracle ordering is not an artifact of the shipped corner. It
  holds at all 12 cells and reverses nowhere.
- Its *size* is not stable: +0.010 to +0.047, a factor of five. Quote the range
  or quote the shipped cell with its regime; do not quote +0.025 as if it were
  the gap.
- The cold-start mechanism is conditional. At `shock_scale` 0.5 the filter never
  learns coverage (reach 0.46) and the gap is small for a different reason.
- Nothing here speaks to a hypergamy conclusion at low β/α, and the stated reason
  for that ("δ is inert below 2") is not supported by measurement at the shipped
  population. The honest statement is that δ moves no headline in this grid.

## Quoting these numbers

Every cell is a mean over five seeds with a BCa interval, in
`../sensitivity.csv`. The seed spread says the simulation is stable, not that the
result generalizes: the model has no empirical calibration. See
`docs/STATS.md` §4.

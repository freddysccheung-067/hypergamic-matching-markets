\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A learned ranker between the two sides of a matching market concentrates
exposure relative to an oracle, and the concentration is a start-up cost rather
than a standing feature. That is the paper's finding, and it is a finding about
\emph{when} the measurement is taken as much as about what is measured: at the
shipped hundred-epoch horizon the gap is $\gapBaseTwenty$ over twenty seeds and
looks like a floor; at
three hundred epochs a pre-declared equivalence test at a $0.002$ margin puts it
inside the margin. The hundred-epoch figure is therefore far from where the
series settles, though the ratio between the two is not itself a measured
quantity. A literature that reports single-horizon comparisons cannot distinguish
a transient from a bias, and this mechanism supplies a case where the distinction
is decisive. What is not established is the limit: a flat tail is compatible with
a very slow decay, and separating them needs a horizon this study did not run.

Three qualifications travel with it, and one of them is a retraction. The
ordering holds in every cell of a twelve-cell sweep, on both interval
constructions, but its size varies fivefold, so no single number is "the" gap.
The mechanism offered, that the filter learns whom it can represent, is supported
by a warm start that leaves only $\warmDecayGapZero$ percent of the gap's decay in
place; it is not separated from the plain accumulation of gradient steps, and the
two other tests run here could not test it at all. And the institutional companion does not survive
where the paper runs: an interior optimum exists in the gate regime that ignores
the market's own outcomes, and at the shipped gate no cell of forty beats the
global market at any size or cost. A companion result that requires a
configuration the rest of the paper does not use is a result about that
configuration.

What would move this from a model result toward a claim about markets is
calibration, and the paper does not attempt it. What would settle the mechanism is
an intervention that separates gradient steps from data, which the warm start does
not. Until then the results bound what
this mechanism can do under a stated utility, and the bound is the contribution.

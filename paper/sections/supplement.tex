\section{Supplementary notes}
\label{app:supplement}

\paragraph{S1. The clearing ceiling of a common list.} If every proposer's list is
the same $k$ acceptors, only those $k$ acceptors receive proposals, and each holds
at most one, so at most $k$ pairs form and the clearing rate is at most $2k/N$.
The same holds when every acceptor's list is the same $k$ proposers. Lists that
differ across agents remove the bound. At round 0, when the ranker hands every
agent on a side nearly the same list, the learned ranker's clearing rate is
$\mcrZero$; at round 299 it is $\mcrFinal$, against $2k/N = 0.04$.

\paragraph{S2. Five seeds against twenty.} Seeds 42 to 46 alone, the five seeds of the
project's earlier results, are a poor basis for an interval. On the exploratory set of the canonical
run, seeds 42 to 61 at round 99, the standard deviation over those five seeds is
$\sdFiveGap$ for the gap against $\sdTwentyGap$ over all twenty, and
$\sdFiveLoss$ for algorithmic loss against $\sdTwentyLoss$. Ordered from the
smallest standard deviation, the block 42 to 46 has rank \rankFiveGap{} of the
sixteen contiguous five-seed blocks for the gap and rank \rankFiveLoss{} for
algorithmic loss: an interval on algorithmic loss built on those five seeds alone
would have been about \sdLossRatio{} times too narrow. Every interval in this paper
is computed over ten or twenty seeds.

\paragraph{S3. Two interval constructions.} At twenty seeds the BCa bootstrap
interval for the round-99 gap is $[\gapNinetyNineBcaLo, \gapNinetyNineBcaHi]$
against the $t$ interval $[\gapNinetyNineLo, \gapNinetyNineHi]$. The two agree to
$\bcaAgree$, which is why the paper reports $t$ intervals throughout and BCa as a
check.

\paragraph{S4. The warm-start measure.} The share of decay surviving a warm start
can be computed on levels or gaps, from round 0 or from each arm's peak. A
level is not a gap: it includes the oracle's own Gini, which falls over the run.
The primary measure here, the gap on the round-0 basis, was fixed after all four
candidates had been computed on earlier five-seed data, and the registration says
so. The four constructions
differ for two reasons. On levels, the oracle's own Gini falls between round 0 and
the end in both arms alike, and a level difference counts that fall in numerator
and denominator. On a peak basis, the warm arm climbs for a few rounds after its
informed start, and measuring it from that later and lower peak discards the part
of the effect the pretraining produced. The four values on forty seeds are in
Table~\ref{tab:warm}.
